Ankle mobility is needed for multiple movements, including standing, walking, jumping, getting up from a chair, and going up and down stairs. To perform these movements, plantarflexion and dorsiflexion are required. Limited plantarflexion and dorsiflexion can contribute to injury and is associated with conditions such as foot drop .
