Cerebral palsy is a nonprogressive condition, but that doesn’t mean mobility needs stay the same over time. For adults in their 30s, 40s, and beyond, accumulated stress on bones and muscles can take a toll, causing pain and stiffness – and the need for different ways to support mobility.
